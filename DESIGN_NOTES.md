# Zenith Club — design rationale

## What the redesign solves

The supplied member screenshots have a clear five-tab structure but show large empty states, repeated outlined cards, a long account menu and little product presence. The redesign keeps the recognisable Home, Book, Tap, Play and Me model, while giving each area a more useful first screen.

The main screen brings the band, member pass and next booking together. Book makes search, sport, date and member pricing visible before a form. Tap treats the band as a tangible product with colour changes and a separate 3D studio. Play gives the member concrete games to browse. Me groups identity and progress beside account actions.

## Visual direction

Quiet clubhouse luxury: warm ivory, ink/forest, soft sage, cognac accents, fine serif headings and a restrained sans-serif interface. This draws from Zenith's identity and the clarity, hierarchy and generous touch controls associated with Apple's interface guidance, without copying an Apple interface.

Desktop uses a fixed club navigation rail. Mobile has a dedicated composition, compact header and floating bottom navigation. The mobile hero is rearranged, not simply narrowed. Light and dark appearances use the same structure.

## Product presentation

The supplied terry sweatband is deliberately represented as a broad textile cuff. It is not recast as a silicone wristband, fitness tracker or smartwatch. Generated reference-based product imagery provides the photographic layer; a procedural Three.js component provides true rotation and material depth when WebGL is available.

## Demonstration boundary

The demo label remains visible, while each important transactional action explains its immediate effect. Bookings are local demo records; games and friends are fictional; no payment or original authentication is connected. This makes the experience testable without implying access to live inventory or membership infrastructure.

The public marketing site was used only as a reference. No new shop, checkout or marketing landing page was built.
